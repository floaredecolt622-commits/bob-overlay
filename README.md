# Bob

Bob is a tiny Android pet that floats above your apps. Drag him around, bring him his favorite snacks, dress him up, or see how many hat trickshots you can land in a row.

**[Download Bob v7.8 for Android](bob-v7.8.apk)** · [What's new](CHANGELOG.md) · [Older versions](versions.zip/)

## Get started

1. Download and open the APK on a device with Android 8.0 or newer. Android may ask you to allow your browser or file manager to install the APK.
2. Open **Bob**, tap **Start**, and allow **Display over other apps**. Return to Bob if Android takes you to Settings.
3. Tap Bob for a reaction, drag him to a new spot, or give him a quick flick to watch him slide. To hide him, return to the app and tap **Stop**.

## Snacks, naps, and outfits

- **Feed Bob.** Use 🍔 in the app to pick four snacks. Press and hold the floating Bob to bring up his snack picker, then drag a snack to him while he's awake. If he's asleep, wait for him to wake up.
- **Let Bob ask for food.** Open 🕒🍎 and turn on **Bob asks for snacks**. Choose a random interval or set specific times. Bob asks for one of the snacks you've selected.
- **Dress him up.** Open 🎩 to combine accessories. Bob can wear one loose hat or crown at a time. If you want the hat to stay on, turn off **Headwear can fall off** in Settings.
- **Catch a falling hat.** You can drag a dropped hat back to Bob, throw it, or wait for a little star to rescue it.
- **Try a trickshot.** Throw a detached hat or crown so it bounces off the floor or a screen edge and lands back on Bob. The bounce must happen after you throw. A miss or a throw without a bounce breaks your streak.

## Quests and levels

Turn on **Enable leveling system** in ⚙️ Settings, then tap ⭐ to check your XP and daily quests. Feeding Bob, playing, answering snack requests, and landing trickshots can earn XP. Each level takes 100 XP, with a daily cap of 60 XP.

Four quests are picked each day from 1,000 variations and refresh at local midnight. For the **Mega Quest**, keep Bob in the top quarter of the usable screen and land two consecutive trickshots. Missed quests leave your XP intact.

| Milestone | What you earn |
| --- | --- |
| Levels 1, 5, 10, 20 | Tiny Pal, Snack Buddy, Best Bud, and Bob Legend titles |
| Levels 3, 5, 8, 12, 20 | Star Hat, Witch Hat, Rainbow Hat, Cloud Cap, and Golden Topper |
| Levels 21–25 | Cozy Scarf, Halo, Tiny Wings, Mini Backpack, and Heart Pin |

## Updates and older versions

Open ⚙️ **Settings → Change versions** to check GitHub for a newer APK or find older APKs. Open **Settings → Changelog** to see what changed.

Open the downloaded APK in Android’s Files app to install the update.

Use **Back up Bob** to save your progress and settings before switching versions. Use **Restore Bob** when you want to load that backup. **Legacy APKs** opens the older downloads.

## Help and downloads

Tap **?** in Bob for the in-app help. If Bob's play area looks wrong, use **Calibrate screen** in Settings. You can also turn tap vibration off there. You can also find the [source archive](source%20code.zip) and the [older APKs](versions/) here.
