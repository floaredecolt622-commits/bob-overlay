# Bob’s changelog

## v7.8 · Updates through Files

GitHub still checks for updates and downloads APKs. Open a saved APK from Android’s Files app to install it.

## v7.7 · A home for Bob’s updates

Bob has more things to say when a fast drag makes him dizzy. Bob can check GitHub for updates. Choose Skip to dismiss that release or Continue to read its notes. In Settings, Change versions lists compatible releases and opens the archive for older ones. Back up Bob’s progress, accessories and settings before switching to an older compatible build, then restore them there.

## v7.6 · Back to single player

Multiplayer rooms were removed. The solo overlay, snacks, accessories, hat rescues and daily quests carried forward.

## v7.5 · Solo animations and daily draws

Solo Bob regained his own drawing code. The daily quest picker began saving each day’s selection and avoiding the previous day’s names.

## v7.4 · Snacks in shared rooms

Room snacks gained a sparkling entrance, drag animations, sliding throws and feeding on contact. Bobs could bump into each other.

## v7.1–v7.3 · Familiar faces in multiplayer

Bobs brought their accessories into rooms. Shared play gained more of solo Bob’s animations, tap reactions, drag and slide movement, and the host’s hold-to-feed picker.

## v7.0 · Host from a phone

The host could run a room in Termux and share a public tunnel address and room code with a friend.

## v6.9 · Bob’s neighborhood

Multiplayer rooms let friends move and interact with the same family of Bobs across devices. The host could share a code and remove a player.

## v6.6–v6.8 · Five more ways to dress Bob

The scarf, halo, tiny wings, mini backpack and heart pin joined the wardrobe. Later builds in this group placed them at levels 21 through 25 and showed the unlock requirements.

## v6.3–v6.5 · New quests each day

Bob gained four daily picks from 1,000 named quest variations and a progress bar on the main screen. Trickshot streaks called for consecutive catches. A missed throw broke the streak.

## v6.0–v6.2 · More hats and a tidier home

The level reward collection grew to include the Witch Hat, Cloud Cap and Golden Topper. The help button moved to the top corner.

## v5.9 · Meet the Mega Quest

The fourth challenge became the Mega Quest. The help menu explained feeding, progression and trickshots. Level rewards introduced new hats while the original accessories stayed available.

## v5.6–v5.8 · Levels and trickshots

Optional progression added XP, level titles and daily tasks. After an early two-surface challenge, a successful trickshot could use one bounce. The fourth quest asked for two trickshots with Bob near the top of the screen.

## v5.3–v5.5 · When Bob gets hungry

Bob could ask for one of his chosen snacks with a flashing food bubble. Players could choose a random time interval or add individual times of day, with an optional star to help feed him.

## v4.9–v5.2 · Trickshot landings and a Santa hat

Blinking and yawning appeared briefly, then were removed. Thrown headwear could land back on Bob’s head. The Santa hat drifted down more slowly with a sway.

## v4.7–v4.8 · Feel the shake

Tapping Bob could make the phone vibrate. A later tap vibration followed his shaking rhythm. Settings let players turn it off.

## v4.1–v4.6 · A little gravity

Loose headwear gained gravity and throwing physics. Players could turn drops on or off and calibrate Bob’s play area around the phone controls. The star learned to pat a sleeping Bob twice. Accessory combinations joined the wardrobe.

## v3.0–v4.0 · Dress-up and the helping star

Hats, a bow tie, headphones, a flower and a crown gave Bob his first wardrobe. The fit changed as the collection grew. Dropped hats could be returned by hand or collected by a star that flew in from the screen edge. Snacks gained sparkles.

## v2.6–v2.9 · Snack time

Snacks gained movement and a pop-in animation. A sleeping Bob explained why he couldn’t eat. Players could choose four snacks from the food list and reset them to the original selection.

## Early Bob · After v1.0 and before v2.6

Bob gained dragging, tap bubbles, dizziness after fast movement, and sliding throws that bounced off screen edges. Holding Bob opened up feeding, with a growing ring and a four-snack picker. Individual patch dates for these changes are unknown.

## v1.0 · The first overlay

A little white circle with a :D face floated above other apps. Tap him for a startled shake, or leave him alone to see him fall asleep with drifting Zs.
